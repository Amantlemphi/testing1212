Who We Are

Rooted in Faith,
Growing in Botswana

The Apostolic Faith Church of Portland, Oregon – Botswana Branch is a community of believers dedicated to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Founded on the principles of the original 1906 Azusa Street revival, we uphold the full Gospel message of salvation, sanctification, and the baptism of the Holy Spirit.

We serve communities across Botswana with love, prayer, and dedication to God's Word — welcoming all who seek a deeper relationship with Christ.
